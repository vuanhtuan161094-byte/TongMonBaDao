## Evidence classes (mandatory tags in reports)
- **VERIFIED_SOURCE**: exact path/content/hash checked.
- **OBSERVED_FRAME**: actual image/video time or frame inspected; say what visible.
- **INFERRED_ART**: proposed explanation from evidence (not source implementation).
- **UNVERIFIED**: missing prefab/material/frames/rights linkage.
- **ORIGINAL_PROPOSAL**: a new TMB method, not a recovered JX method.
- **GODOT_CAPTURED**: actual movie generated by Godot/QA with exact SHA.
- **OWNER_VISUAL_APPROVED**: only direct owner confirmation, never CI inference.

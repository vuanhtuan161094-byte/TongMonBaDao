Keep each effect centered inside its cell, readable when scaled to 96 pixels wide, with crisp alpha edge and rich painterly internal detail. Row 1 Cửu Cung Tỏa Lộ: restrained antique-gold nine-square compass seal that becomes parallel locking road-lines, then a compact geometric lock impact, then dissolving gold strokes. Row 2 Bát Môn Phong Hành: eightfold jade-green wind gate, distinct curved ribbon gust opening into a moving gate, then a sharp wind clasp impact, then separated fading brushstrokes. Row 3 Huyền Thạch Trấn Vị: heavy earth-brown floating slate fragments around a square stone ward, descending into a grounded stone pin impact, then crumbling into a few dusty flecks. Row 4 Tứ Tượng Hãm Trận: four distinct beast-aspect ribbons in vermilion, blue-green, ivory and aged gold forming a cross-shaped trap, converging into a four-point pin, then fading as four separate trails. Do not repeat one generic orb/ring with changed colors. Do not draw a scenic background. Maintain large visible transparent negative space around every sprite.

**Transparency edit prompt**

> Edit this exact image as a game-ready RGBA sprite atlas. Preserve every VFX sprite's colors, shapes, detail, and its current 4-column by 4-row arrangement. Remove the entire pale cream/white background, haze, grid mat, and all backdrop pixels from every cell. Make all pixels outside the painted effects fully transparent alpha=0, including every gutter. Keep delicate smoke/ribbon pixels only where they are actually part of the effects; remove the uniform cream matte and white cast around every edge. Output a clean transparent PNG with no new backdrop and no changes to the sprite artwork.

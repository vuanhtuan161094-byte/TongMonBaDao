## Binding R6 design: luminous martial energy with clear identity
**Visual semantic:** “Đệ tử tụ huyết khí sáng trong ở quyền → ép thành một khối quyền kình có bốn gờ năng lượng gợi đốt tay → phóng lực ngắn từ tư thế quyền → nổ một điểm va chạm sắc, rồi để lại ba đường huyết mạch.” A compact **ENERGY-DEFINED fist pressure shape**, not a photographic/anatomical fist cutout.

**Material hierarchy:**
1. **HOT CORE** small near-white/ivory blush (bright enough at 1×, avoid wide bloom) at knuckle/contact tip.
2. **ENERGY BODY** ruby–carmine light with saturated colored RGB but translucency, inner flowing cross-strands and **sharply readable knuckle-pressure silhouette**. No muddy dark red solid or continuous opaque rectangle.
3. **EDGE / HALO** soft pink-violet/red-violet alpha fringe, asymmetric and short; 1–2 slim directional tails only when they define force/momentum.
4. **CONTACT** split white-hot center → small red/pink crescent/radial shards and narrow local displacement of energy, immediate springback; don't make bright circular UI ring or an obscuring full-screen flash.
5. **MARK** 3 original short angled luminous meridian cuts, compact, distinct from generic poison, follows only 1 target for **declared** 6s after real receipt (LAB timing is illustrative).

**Real original authored phase poses are required**. Suggested motion storyboard (exact pose count derived from motion, not artificial quotas):
| VFX phase | LAB reference interval, not Combat V2 hit timestamp | The actual new source art must CHANGE |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Accumulate | 0.10–0.30 | translucent fragmented energy converges to caster-hand pivot, 4 knuckle points implied |
| 1. Compress | 0.30–0.45 | hot ivory spine shortens/densifies, halo cinches, directional force charges |
| 2. Strike release | 0.45–0.62 | asymmetric luminous fist-pressure silhouette **thrusts** at melee visual range, not an image teleporting from distant caster |
| 3. Strike peak | 0.62–0.90 | 4 short luminous knuckle ridges and tapered body visually connected through force direction, weight transfer |
| 4. Impact | 0.90–1.04 | sharp localized bright collision, asymmetric crack/light recoil at selected moving target torso, not broad red opaque block |
| 5. Rebound/fade | 1.04–1.27 | light body retracts/disperses into short filaments, leave target visible |
| 6. Mark | contact+6 seconds | compact 3 cut meridian glyph with transparent core, target-relative fade, not a second fake damage effect |

A nonliteral clenched-knuckle **energy pressure silhouette** is permissible, but pure squiggles, 2 abstract ribbons, dark red opaque gore, literal hand sprite, or a glowing sphere are NOT. **No fake multi-hit, stun, bleeding/DoT, projectile or homing mechanics**.

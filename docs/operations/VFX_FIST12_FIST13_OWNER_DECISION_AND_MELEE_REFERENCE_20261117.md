## Cross-game references — facts vs observations vs proposals
1. **Dungeon Fighter Online** — screen-observable in online gameplay screenshots: punches/slashes and target contacts create **directional high-contrast jagged impacts** near the receiving actor, with sharply varying widths and a readable character. This is a visual observation, not a claim about Neople's private implementation technology. Reference image: https://www.mobile01.com/topicdetail.php?f=284&t=3325102 ; another: https://multiplayer.it/recensioni/83972-dungeon-fighter-online-il-beatem-up-si-traveste-da-mmorpg.html. Borrow *ideas*: asymmetric punch shapes, compact sparks aimed along the blow, overlapping successive contact silhouettes. Do NOT copy game pixels.
2. **Guilty Gear -Strive-** — Arc System Works states hand-crafted expressive animation, per-part shading/light control and production collaboration; visual collision emphasizes strongly staged poses and bright attack feedback. First-party studio interview via Epic: https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/how-guilty-gear--strive--hits-an-ultra-combo-with-groundbreaking-visuals-and-gameplay. Developer's Backyard also reports the studio had to work on character visibility amid rich FX: https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/news/post-1092/. Borrow *ideas*: clear attack silhouette, contrast hierarchy, concentrated frames of impact, don't engulf fighters. Not a Godot implementation claim.
3. **Riot/League of Legends VFX guidelines** — primary sources emphasize legibility, minimizing visual noise, thematic identity and focus matched to mechanic. https://www.riotgames.com/en/artedu/visual-effects ; https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/ . Transfer as **universal VFX design principles**, not claim LoL uses same melee pipelines as Godot.
4. **Open-source Godot components** — REEBOO stylized 2D lite supplies reusable hit spark and slash arc with shader/particles, under MIT, https://github.com/REEBO0/godot-stylized-2d-vfx-lite ; fit as **minor hit-spark reference**, NOT unique master art. Godot official AnimatedSprite2D and SpriteFrames describe true source sprite animation: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/2d_sprite_animation.html ; Godot GPUParticles2D docs https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_gpuparticles2d.html . REEBOO shape must not substitute for hand-authored hero.
5. **Game Feel Lab**, https://store.godotengine.org/asset/codingquests/game-feel-lab/ , demonstrates separating knockback, hitstun, hit-stop, camera shake, sparks, white flash and squash. This is a prototype reference to investigate; **do not enable real gameplay hit-stop or knockback** under view-only authorization. A local non-gameplay QA slow/freeze may be simulated visually only. Settings must remain accessible for photosensitivity/reduced FX.

## Recommended FIST12 *visual-only* 3-pulse choreography for one MARK (subject to G3.5 / owner review)
A. **Anticipation 0–0.45s** aligned to declared cast: narrow garnet-white compression around real caster-hand anchor; character punch animation only if independently authored frame-by-frame. No giant aura.
B. **First visual punch accent**: short narrow raked forward fist-edge, angular contact white-point, low-amplitude warm-red lateral chips; brief limited visibility. Not counted as a separate damage event.
C. **Second visual punch accent**: different lower-to-upper or opposite diagonal, medium-width tapered sheet and subdued secondary edge light; no generic radial ring. Not a second gameplay hit.
D. **Finisher visual punch accent**: clearly the heaviest, compressed true-painted fist crest and a 2–3 frame white-hot point with 3–6 asymmetric directional hit-spark flecks, fading pressure sheet. One selected moving foe. Single mark application remains authoritative and runtime-receipt-gated; LAB simulated timeline only, do not infer multiple hit_count.
E. **Break-meridian aftermark**: shrink to a small distinctive broken-fist/meridian icon on **foe chest or near shoulder**, not over the head/face. Persist exactly declared 6s, remain attached as target walks. Separate from brief punch/contact pose, and fade without red cloud.

**Spatial layer order (typical 2D LAB):** background → discreet near-ground contact dust → back-facing pressure sheets behind foe → drawn actor/foe → front localized impact hot core → sparse spark tips → small 6s mark. The main pressure form should have a coherent directional silhouette even with bloom disabled; not solely glow. Keep true-alpha isolated sprite sources and 3 distinct action keys preserved by SHA; any temporal blending must not wash out the hit key.

**Contact timing:** the public catalogue doesn't specify actual Combat V2 receipt timestamps. All detailed punch events above are *candidate art-only choreography*, not authoritatively encoded attack counts. Confirm G0 actual receipt to select mark attachment phase; LAB synthetic samples may be shown with labels. Avoid fake knockback/physical hitstop in game. Do not confuse 0.45s cast, 6s status and QA movie duration.

## Practical Godot technical breakdown
- Separate `AnimatedSprite2D` or Sprite2D+AnimationPlayer for hand/pressure/contact/mark key textures; distinct SpriteFrames per stage with explicit cell anchors and per-frame timing. A project-specific `VfxPresentation` node follows current enemy anchor, does not own HP/status.
- `CanvasItemMaterial` MIX for pigment; restrained additive glowing core + soft radial falloff; sparse `GPUParticles2D` for tiny flecks only. Do not use hard-edged code-drawn neon polygon for the primary punch. Favor 1 or 2 localized light accents vs whole-scene bloom.

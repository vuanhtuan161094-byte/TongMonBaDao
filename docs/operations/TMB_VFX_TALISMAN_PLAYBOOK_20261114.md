### Why a line lightning skill still missed a mobile monster on screen
The TAL04/TAL05 older LABs drew the enemies and contact scars at hardcoded screen coordinates. PvE itself interpolates positions by actor identity (see `pve_jx_continuous_world_view_v1.gd`) and the existing VFX adapter `pve_jx_sword_vfx_adapter_v1.gd` already binds Combat V2's `skill_cast`/`skill_hit`/`hit` events to `actor_id` and `target_id`; LAB position was the visual defect. A spell can **visually track the same authorized moving target ID**, but cannot select enemies, guarantee a hit, widen a LINE attack or create damage from a VFX cue.

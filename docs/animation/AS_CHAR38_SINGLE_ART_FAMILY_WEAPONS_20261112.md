## 2. A single visual DNA for all 38 design groups

- Shadow Pixel-Chibi dark Xianxia; adult proportions 3–3.5 heads, consistent face-language by sex without swapping an individual identity. Not half realistic-anime and half pixel.
- Shared low-resolution pixel-cluster rule at exported game size, crisp nearest filtering; no arbitrary downscaled illustration, smoothing, per-frame pixel-density change, or random ornaments.
- Same head-to-body ratio, fixed upper-left key light, dark charcoal/violet shadows, three to five major value families per material; different sect palette accents only in controlled role hues.
- Stable hairstyle, face/brows/eyes, costume seam layout, accessories, hand dominance, weapon design, scales, foot pivot and silhouette across ALL frames, directions and clips.
- Default on-screen map export 128×128 RGBA, logical foot anchor (64,104), visible character 60–75 px at 1×. Combat-specific 160×192, foot (80,179) is a SEPARATE export and must be registered independently.
- Authentic transparency, no baked checkerboard/background, weapon-specific projectile, cast ring, slash trail or summoned entity inside body texture.
- Approval is per ORIGINAL named identity and recorded image SHA-256. Re-styling by another generation or color swapping is NOT identity preservation.

## 3. How to avoid the previous GIF identity drift

1. Make ONE approved front full-body master per novice identity (male/female). Freeze exact original PNG hash and proportions. Do not infer approval from a later master with similar colors.
2. Author four views from that **image-conditioned same identity**, with both actual side views, a true back view, anchored feet, correct asymmetry and consistent hand carrying the weapon.
3. Preserve editable pixel source separated as body/hair/robe/forearm/hand/weapon, and per-view grip and z-order, plus occluded body/robe reconstruction. Do not require runtime Skeleton2D/Bone2D.
4. Build storyboards for Idle/Walk/Cast/Attack/Hit/Death. Produce action-specific **key pixel poses + reviewed in-betweens** from the same locked character source, not independently invented full-frame characters. 8-frame loops are examples, never quotas. No whole-image warp or moving a still to simulate walking.
5. Every generated GIF has to pass a visual frame-by-frame identity gate. Reject immediately for changed sex/eyes, hair ornaments, sword-fan substitution, costume redraw, shifted foot, cloned frames, weapon hand teleport, mismatched light or smoothed rendering. Compare 1× at 60–75 px and nearest-neighbor 6×.
6. Do NOT call a GIF production animation until decomposed into transparent registered actual PNG frames, separated weapon/FX layers and imported into Godot 4.7.2 with authentic playback.
7. Record original master SHA, generation/edit route, every frame source, alignment, anchors, PNG hashes, atlas index, animation speed/events, alpha edge metrics, true frame visibility and QA evidence. Unverified clip stays PENDING.

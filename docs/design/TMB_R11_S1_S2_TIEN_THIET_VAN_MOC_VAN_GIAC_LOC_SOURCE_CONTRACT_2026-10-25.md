## 5. Ba ống giao dịch S2 đề xuất
**Ore (M) & Fiber (F):** [start] có node thế hệ chưa thu + worker thật không đang có active job khác → reserve source+worker → travel/work/haul theo **sim ticks và job** → freeze qty/ID (không từ UI) → kiểm `ResourceRegistry`/Warehouse overflow → một commit logic: tăng canonical stock, đánh dấu node generation settled, ghi source receipt, grant profession XP nếu được author đúng nguồn → schedule respawn theo tick. Nếu worker bị Injury hoặc interrupted khi trước delivery, giữ/giải phóng reservation theo policy persisted, nhưng **không đã credit Warehouse** trước rồi lại credit khi delivery. Một chu kỳ physical source → tối đa một receipt.
**Beast (N):** monster actor thuộc world/camp/slot/gen thật, HP do Combat V2 sở hữu; death terminal freeze một source result; preflight registered ID/amount and stock; commit one unique life key → credit one common material only if policy and roll cho phép → mark settled and defer generation+1 until all effects persisted. Multiple party contributors do not generate multiple reward rolls. Camera unload/offscreen/Save replay no extra credits.
**Ownership collision:** Current V2 whole-encounter reward paid once by `encounter_id`. S2 must use a **separate future World-source award owner with feature flags and reconciliation to legacy encounter** (not an independent parallel loot generator). Either new World mode owns per-kill source while old encounter packets remain unchanged in legacy mode, OR migrate source entitlement to one unified ledger per reward class with evidence. No automatic both-mode payout; migration/source overlap test is mandatory.

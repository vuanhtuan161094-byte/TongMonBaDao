## 2. Five independent operations, never collapse their results
| Operation [P] | What changes | Does NOT change | Failure/keep [P] |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ENHANCE` Cường hóa | one bounded `enhancement_level`; raises **approved base item stat component** (not entire Disciple stats) | grade, equipment realm, affix IDs, set identity, Chân Văn | failed attempt spends committed fee/material, keeps existing item/+level |
| `REFINE_VALUE` Tinh luyện | numerical value on ONE selected existing legal affix ID within its range | number/identity of affixes, phẩm, cảnh giới | roll after payment; choose keep OLD or accept NEW, never refund on reject |
| `REROLL_AFFIX` Tẩy luyện | replace identity of ONE chosen line from its legitimate slot A/B/C pool | line capacity, Chân Văn, item generation | immutable new candidate; keep OLD vs NEW, cost paid once regardless |
| `PROMOTE_GRADE` Thăng phẩm | grade `g→g+1` only with authorized template/source/catalyst; increases capacity for legal affixes | cultivation realm or Chân Văn inscription | fail consumes attempts but **not** current grade/item; no free perfect affixes |
| `PROMOTE_REALM` Thăng bậc cảnh giới trang bị | item realm index `r→r+1`, recompute canonical base per legal item template/realm; preserve lawful investments | phẩm does not auto-increase, no lower-world source into Thần | fail keeps item/current realm; high-rank recipe required |
| `INHERIT` Kế thừa | transfer **bounded investment discount** to eligible target and destroy donor item once | does not clone original grade/affix/Chân Văn, source material or donor | prefer deterministic legal transfer; exact percentage/cost open |

Tinh luyện **trị số** and Tẩy luyện **dòng/identity** cannot use identical permission: first always retains affix ID, second may change it subject to grade/slot/source/duplicate caps. Grade promotion only opens *capacity*, not guaranteed 6 ideal lines. Individual Chân Văn uses separate later contract, not affix reroll.

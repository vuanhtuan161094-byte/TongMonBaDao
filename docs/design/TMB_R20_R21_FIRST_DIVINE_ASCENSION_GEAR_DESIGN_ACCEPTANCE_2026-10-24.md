## 3. Critical dependency diamond: Forge Lv30 vs Lv75 [OWNER MUST DECIDE]
**Canonical building directions [LOCKED DIRECTION, not code]:** Lv1 basic, Lv10 auto repair, Lv20 material processing, **Lv30 monster-material forging**, Lv50 deeper properties, **Lv75 advanced sets/special chance**, Lv100 mastery. `BuildingState.level` and `worker_group_id` exist, but source main BuildingSystem does NOT show an implemented end-to-end 10-slot `eqv2_*` R21 Forge contract, queue/cost/ability that can be marked PASS. **Path A — RECOMMENDED non-softlocking design [P]:** Lv30 can forge **R21 BASIC gear only if the specific R21 common recipe is learned and real R21 ore/fiber/beast were earned**; building level does not grant material tier or grade quality by itself. Lv75 remains advanced set/rare-line/Chân Văn-work capability after its own genuine investment. An R20 Sect with Lv30 and accessible R21 frontier can acquire first R21 blank items without first needing Lv75. Protects late-tier chase because ordinary blank R21 ≠ premium grade9–12. Requires owner approval of this narrow Lv30 cross-generation **basic-only** exception, not all Thần recipes.
**Path B — strict Lv75 for ANY R21 output [P, BLOCK until proven]:** only acceptable if there is a complete R20-and-lower-paid actual building upgrade path Lv30→75 (every upgrade recipe material ID + source and upgrade duration available before first R21 item). Then R20 team levels Forge to75, mines/farms R21 frontier with old gear, researches basic R21 library and forges. If even ONE Lv30→75 upgrade stage requires the R21 gear/Thần-only boss relic or a post-R21 profession job to produce its own material, **BLOCKED**, no compensation by auto granting Lv75, and do not falsely count pilot Lv75 direction as functioning building.
**Unacceptable Path C:** silently create initial R21 item by promotion of old R20 after a levelup, free starter crate, Body-Gate Seal use, NPC sale at unregistered market, fake offline income or “power=R21 but same tier item ID”. This breaks source and Save invariants and is NOT accepted under either A or B.
**Profession qualification [P]:** true `forging` proficiency `Level=floor(sqrt(XP/24))`, `speed=1+.01×Level` affects *work duration*, not grade/quantity without authored trial outcome. Lv30/75 are **building levels**, not worker profession level nor item realm; one worker cannot mine during a real Forge operation (One Active Job). If the only qualified worker is injured or already hunting, the job must stay WAITING/PAUSED and preserve material reservations; a workforce reallocation cannot grant XP or two jobs.
**Facility prerequisite source matrix must be separately authored:** per-building `level_from→level_to` costs only IDs available to a previous-tier party, real build-time duration and worker/resource rules; a non-Boss accessible ore+fiber path must remain after upgrade resource expenditures.

## 0. Authority boundary: product LOCKS vs proposed monster AI

### OWNER-LOCKED
- Persistent PvE **Evil Hunter Tycoon style** on one living flat hunting map: **no 90-second battle**, no finite encounter chains, no wave-WIN/claim/reset; actors fight, die, drop, and respawn **independently** while other creatures keep moving/fighting.
- Combat V2 is sole authority for hit/range, attack speed, skills/cast/cooldowns, HP changes, crit, reductions, RNG/damage. It may be refactored to serve living world but no cloned or new combat formula.
- Monster categories **normal / elite / boss**, multiple teams in same map, shared one HP for each real monster instance; player can observe free-camera pan/zoom without stopping world. No pathfinding/navmesh/terrain obstacles or physical maze; ground/flowers/vegetation are decoration.
- Death source generation/kill is **exactly once** and route to canonical Sect ownership/rewards; no duplicate legacy encounter payout. Downed disciple uses injury **0/1/120 scheduler ticks**, no permanent disciple death, One Active Job protection.
- Content scaling is data-driven, not product-limited to B2 3 map zones/5 recipes. Actual content/drop-rate/story/skills must have authored sources, not generated by designer assumptions.

### DESIGN OPTIONS, NOT LOCKED
- Detection/sight radius, defend-home radius/leash length, roaming area, how to choose between nearby hostile disciples, how/when monster forgets target, how a monster resets HP when returning home, chase speed/retarget cadence, anti-dogpile and max engaged mobs per team, terrain edge handling.
- Monster behavioral archetypes, Elite affixes, Boss encounter phases/telegraphs, boss leash/heal rules, attack encounter contribution and loot split between teams, spawn population targets, boss reset or exclusivity, player risk settings and offline-step optimization.
- Production schema/API names, substep evaluation schedule, strict actor capacity per chunk, boss content formulas (which would require separate Combat V2 approved designs).

**Default architecture recommendation:** solve V1 with **simple deterministic aggro + bounded chase + safe return**, no global Threat stat or MMO pathfinding. Elite/Boss use the **same Combat V2 mechanics** with their legitimate authored stats/skills and different *selection/behavior* configs where evidence supports it. Never mark these defaults LOCKED before owner review.

## 2. Product behavior: convincing living hunting grounds, minimal cleverness

### 2.1 A monster is not a finite battle participant

A monster occupies `MonsterInstance(world_id,region_id,slot_id,generation)` with **one HP, one authoritative position and combat timeline**. Its life persists while the map is viewed or hidden; death retires that generation, pays exactly once, enters respawn cooldown. Returning an actor to home **is not death** and must not create a drop/replace generation. A world with no active hunters may be safely idle, never terminal "defeat".

**Normal example:** 3 Thanh Lang alive. Disciple A arrives, only the nearest eligible wolf detects A (according to proposed perception rules). A attacks; second wolf may discover A when within its own range or legitimately attacked. Wolf 1 dies, its slot schedules respawn. Wolf 2 still fights. A returns for medicine; alive Wolf 2 resumes PATROL/RETURN_HOME; when A' hunter revisits, no wave/battle setup.

**Boss example:** Hắc Lang Vương's single instance HP is shared across two legal hunter teams. It follows its source-authored V2 skills and telegraphed AOE while alive. Team 1 backs off, team 2 continues; no separate Boss copy spawned for team 2; no "Boss died when all parties retreat" or global WIN.

### 2.2 Monster local AI (only decisions, not damage logic)

Recommended **simple monster states**:
```text
WAIT_SPAWN (per spawn slot)
     → IDLE_AT_HOME ↔ LOCAL_WANDER [optional, no RNG from visual]
     → NOTICE (eligible disciple in configured detect region or valid incoming hit)
     → PURSUE (select valid target, move at real Combat V2 move_speed)
     → ATTACK_OR_CAST (actual CombatV2WorldStep: range/cooldowns/cast/skill)
     ↔ RETARGET (target dead, left valid area, threat event/assist)
     → RETURN_HOME (leash/time/owner safety rule; no drop, no respawn)
     → IDLE_AT_HOME
     → DYING (one authoritative HP >0→0) → RESPAWN_WAIT → IDLE_AT_HOME new generation
```
This is **AI intent state** with real combat actor HP/actions held in WorldState. Do not create second authoritative monster HP/clock in AI data. `NOTICE` and `RETARGET` happen at meaningful time/events; `ATTACK_OR_CAST` is not a second attack engine.

**AI conditions:** an actor is eligible only if same live region, HP>0, not already canonical departed, within real configured sensory range or has attacked this monster under permitted retaliation behavior. Distances measured foot-to-foot. Even if a monster can visually see the hero, V2 damage requires legal actual attack range and cast condition. A monster cannot sense across the whole zone merely because actor appears in `battle.actors`.

### 2.3 Bounded pursuit/leash

- Each monster has `spawn_home_xy`, optional authored `wander_zone`, and proposed `leash_region_or_radius`. These are **AI constraints, not physical walls or pathfinding**.
- Normal/Elite/Boss may have different behavior profiles (config not authored yet). On a legal target leaving territory, monster should eventually RETURN_HOME; not chase hunter all over an effectively unbounded zone.
- **OPEN**: when return begins, whether an engaged pending cast finishes and who may hit during return; whether HP is preserved, regens, or resets, and the rate/conditions. Any reset must not emit `Killed` or free loot, must be authoritative and reproducible online/offline.
- **Recommended V1:** stop initiating new attacks after a safe return barrier; return home at canonical speed, validate cast completion rules, do not instantly teleport. Heal/reset-on-return should default **disabled until owner approves combat and abuse contract**, or use a separately authorized source rule. Mark this recommendation PROPOSED.
- Character foot coordinates remain within simple world bounds, camera-only zoom/pan never changes actual world position.

### 2.4 Aggro without creating a new combat stat

Do not add an MMO `Threat` Combat Stat or taunt multiplier by default. Choose attention target using **deterministic eligibility and ranking** (proposed):
1. Authorized recently hitting/hurt caster or current target if still valid and within eligible scope, where retaliation is allowed;
2. Candidate living hunter inside detection region/territory, no hidden/injured-in-sect clones;
3. Stable closest distance to monster foot, with ID tie break;
4. Optional team grouping/mob-type behavioral tags after explicit authored source.
Keep existing target if still legal to avoid retarget jitter. Retarget on death, exit perception/leash, retreat, valid damage stimulus or scheduled reconsider, not per render frame. AI plan never consumes Combat V2 RNG or feeds it via hidden scene/camera calls.

**Dogpile prevention:** Avoid artificial game-breaking infinite aggro chain where all 40 monsters across a zone target the same approaching hunter. Detection and home boundaries naturally limit engagement. If a numerical max attackers/encounter participation budget is needed, that's **OPEN balance**, not mandatory flat cap hiding legitimate Combat V2 hits.

**Many teams:** A monster may legally perceive several teams and choose one personal target; **one monster HP shared**. The other team may still hit it. No per-team monster ownership lock. A party's medicine/healer/guard checks remain Combat V2 current rules; monster does not trigger forced team-wide defeat.

## 3. Tier profiles: normal, elite, boss

| Tier | Distinguishing target behavior (DESIGN) | Existing V2 mechanics to reuse | NOT proven/locked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NORMAL** | Local patrol near home, detect valid targets, pursue into attack range, retreat home after leash. Dies/respawns per slot. | V2 basic attack/interval/move speed, stats/race/element/context, no skill unless authored. | Assist-call, sophisticated packing, special loot chance. |
| **ELITE** | More durable/escalated authored V2 tier; configurable wider sensory/retreat profile if desired. Remains an independent instance/slot; can coexist with normals. | Current B2 `elite` tier and telegraphed AoE when legal. | Universal affixes, crowd-control phases, spawn quota or HP multipliers beyond actual content. |
| **BOSS** | Rare authored source with visible readable wind-up, permission/risk gates for teams, one HP across all participants. Can continue attacking while one team returns. | Current B2 `boss` tier and telegraphed area attack (V2 rule), cast/cooldown, source taxonomy. | Multi-phase enrages, minion spawns, global spawn event, boss chest, guaranteed sets, share-first-tag/last-hit. |

**First implementation should NOT need three separate AI engines.** One monster AI FSM + versioned `behavior_profile_id` with small option fields. Content tags `normal/elite/boss` determine allowed feature set and existing V2 mechanics; custom phases/skills require new authored Combat V2 content approval.

### Boss readability, no render authority

The boss cast schedules an authoritative center/radius/release time. V5 renders telegraph **behind or semi-transparent around characters** without changing radius/timestamp. Zoom/pan and Source A rig availability do not affect boss damage. Missing animation may result in placeholder/fail visual QA, not invented attack or delayed cast. Boss identity (e.g. `410041_1`) requires catalog provenance; do not automatically certify any skeleton as Hắc Lang Vương.

## 4. Spawn/respawn & ecology responsibilities

### 4.1 Reuse B2.3/2.4 director core, replace local pilot assumptions

`SpawnDirectorV1` has real configurable per-slot `respawn_seconds`, `spawn_pool`, generation and deterministic RNG. Production-world bridge should:
- use source-validated `map/zone/slot` definitions and a world-owned **global canonical time**; avoid drifting independent `director.now` and `battle.now`;
- keep current slot state/generation/actor ID/checkpoint in one world snapshot with real party actor state and kill ledger;
- only spawn when content is authorized and capacity available, with stable tie order. If capacity blocks, leave source `DUE_WAITING_CAPACITY`, no extra generations or rolling spawn RNG repeatedly; when it becomes available, decide whether overdue spawn is immediate or next target tick (OPEN scheduling).
- due spawn does not depend on viewport being shown or Sprite2D existing. If no active world/hunt and region hibernates, canonical respawn deadlines still advance, but **no free kill or loot**.
- validate `respawn_seconds>0` and enough space for spawned instances, avoid infinite same-tick loops. **Pilot example 60 seconds is catalog data, not global lock.**
- `ACTIVE_CAP=40` in B2 director is a **pilot limit**; don't immediately raise it to hundreds without performance tests. B3 content-driven world can later use per-zone/chunk active budgets with metrics.
- no invented "wave completes every 90s", `kill 5 normal → force Boss` or guaranteed rare drop without actual content table.

### 4.2 Monster death contract and lifecycle guarantees

Correct order on every HP lethal:
```text
CombatV2WorldStep (HP before >0, after <=0)
  → freeze DomainKilled(world, zone, slot, generation, exact actor instance,
                       source species, participant damage evidence, absolute_step)
  → mark monster no longer targetable
  → record immutable PENDING reward/source version (if authorized)
  → update slot to respawn cooldown using same absolute clock
  → commit eligible rewards and state via one save snapshot
  → publish optional VFX/audio/loot pickup hint
```

- A monster killed by AoE or ordinary hit gets **one** `Killed` with **one** `slot:generation`. Two monsters killed same substep produce **two** independent events/receipts. A previous-generation projectile must not hit the respawned generation at the same XY.
- Already dead/returning-home monster cannot be independently turned into a new reward by stale hit events, UI scroll/capture or resume.
- **A legal retreat/return-home NEVER emits `Killed`**. Differentiate `DESPAWN_NO_REWARD` for invalid/removed content or hibernation from actual HP-zero death.
- Source missing or loot table missing: world may keep valid combat with an explicit blocked/zero-authored-loot receipt policy after owner approval; don't replay a kill with a new future loot table unless stable provenance and migration rule allow.
- Staged reward commit, idempotent receipts, warehouse/unique/personal progress and versioned SaveService are **production gaps**, separate from sandbox `KillReceiptLedgerV1`.
- **Critical code gap:** B2.4 scans `battle.events` capped UI history to discover deaths. Production **cannot do that**. Domain death event must be synchronously captured from authoritative HP transition, independent of VFX buffer. This is an integration test gate, not a UI issue.

### 4.3 Catalog and import interface (not yet schema changes)

Minimal future `MonsterBehaviorProfileDef` candidate:
```yaml
profile_id: author_approved_string
tier: normal | elite | boss
detect_profile: source_reference_or_tunable_config
home_behavior: stationary | local_wander
leash_profile: source_reference_or_tunable_config
retarget_policy: deterministic_reference
return_home_policy: deterministic_reference
combat_skill_catalog_ref: existing Combat V2 skill reference ONLY
```

Keep `species_id` and `monster tier` in canonical monster registry, `spawn_slot` and `spawn_pool` in **B2 Spawn/Loot Studio** authoring, loot source in versioned loot catalog; behavior fields must have **validated schema/migration/known usage** before the editor exposes them. This data structure is *illustrative*, not deployed.

Map → backend: `region_id`, `zone_id`, `flat world bounds`, `spawn anchors foot_xy`. Backend → Studio/V5: `monster_instance_id`, `species visual ID`, `foot_xy`, `facing`, `HP ratio`, `cast/skill events`, `death/respawn render cues`. Only backend owns live HP/target/cast/loot.

## 5. Proposed monster AI API and world-step execution

**Read-only / pure:**
```text
get_monster_behavior_def(content_version, species_id, tier) -> BehaviorProfile or BLOCKED
get_monster_ai_view(world_id, monster_instance_id) -> detached view
validate_target(monster_actor, candidate_disciple, world, policy) -> true/reason
collect_perceived_hunters(monster_actor, world_spatial_query, detection_context)
    -> sorted list of persistent actor IDs
plan_monster_intent(snapshot, monster_instance_id, current_step)
    -> {state, target_id, movement_goal, reason, next_reconsider_step}
```

**Single authoritative owner / actual mutations:**
```text
admit_due_spawn(world_id, validated_spawn_slot_id, absolute_step) -> MonsterInstance
apply_monster_intent(world_id, monster_instance_id, intent) -> ActionProposal
combat_v2_world_step(world_id, absolute_step, ordered_actor_actions) -> DomainEvents
on_authoritative_lethal(event) -> immutable kill receipt + respawn deadline
enter_return_home(world_id, monster_instance_id, reason) -> ReturnStateResult
save_and_restore_world(world_id, canonical_state) -> versioned safe result
```

**Canonical 0.25s frame recommendation** (actual ordering must match battle parity and shared WorldTime contract): due spawn/casts → valid target and AI intent events → Combat V2 actions → authoritative HP0 domain events → slot/reward staged → team safety/return → read-only presentation events. Team-target and monster-target planners run on event/cached reconsider points, not full 1,000–3,000 roster every quarter-second; Combat V2 action stepping remains authoritative.

**No "separate monster combat engine":** Monster AI determines *intent*, not a damage formula. `CombatV2WorldStep` actually executes attacks/heals/guards/casts/cooldowns, preserving ordering from fixed-roster Combat V2 where comparable. Combat V2 skill configuration must be sourced, never inferred from rig art.

## 6. Edge cases and acceptance design

| Case | Correct expected semantics | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter enters one end of large flat map, wolves far away | Wolves outside authored detection stay home; only legally detecting monsters pursue. | Distances foot-to-foot; no infinite-range `_nearest_enemy` acquisition. |
| 3 monsters around 2 teams | Different monsters may select different members; both teams can damage one instance without duplicate HP. | Shared actor index and stable target ID sorting. |
| No hunters remain after all disciple HP0 | **World does not end**; monsters continue legal idle/return/respawn; wounded disciples settle Injury once. | Explicitly fix B2 continuous `DEFEAT if 0 disciple` in later scoped code. |

| Attacker shoots projectile at wolf gen12; wolf dies; gen13 respawns | Old projectile cannot damage gen13; cast cancellation/commit obeys Combat V2. | Generation-aware ID, no ID reuse. |
| Elite telegraphs AoE, hunter retreats | Cast releases at valid V2 timing/location even if camera pans; visual cannot change damage zone. | Cast/range/parity with Combat V2. |
| Two teams focus a Boss | One HP, one death and one Sect kill receipt; personal credit/anti-leech OPEN. | Shared world actor and immutable receipt. |
| Spawn cap full when another slot due | No extra entity or partial slot mutation; due slot waits, deterministic admission later. | Retry idempotence and clock ordering. |
| Three kills before event queue reaches 2400 cap | Three canonical Killed events and receipts preserved independently of VFX array. | No loss due to UI compaction. |
| Load mid-pursuit/mid-cast/respawn waiting | Same target refs, foot XY, cooldown/cast, RNG/respawn deadline and ledger after restore. | Snapshot version and legacy v1/v2 compatibility. |
| Dynamic team disband while boss in cast | Command barrier safe, no duplicate death/loot; keep legal pending projectiles according to authored V2 rules. | Exactly-once and authorization gate. |
| Map view closed/offscreen, Source A rig not converted | Runtime state and rewards unchanged, render may have placeholder or none. | Viewed/unviewed parity. |
| 24h offline, limited CPU budget | No free kills; backlog can be saved, canonical idle/respawn deadlines advance only when consistent with processed authoritative timeline. | Clock/save parity; no event double advancement. |
| Invalid loot table while monster source valid | No fake loot. Reward blocked with evidence, missing source identified; world may continue legal combat. | Fail closed and no reroll/retroactive mint. |
| Abusive back-and-forth ranged pull | Aggro/leash/return policy prevents infinite chase/teleport/cooldown refresh exploit; exact thresholds need owner approval. | Scenario benchmark without invented HP reset. |

**No numerical acceptance thresholds are locked here.** Test simulations may use explicit fixture detect radius 6 units, leash 10 units, respawn 30 seconds, but these are NOT game defaults.

## 8. Open owner decisions, with cautious recommendations

1. **Aggro:** basic proximity + legitimate retaliation; no universal Threat stat. Recommend balanced first version with one target remembered until invalid, no per-frame target flutter. **OPEN numerical radius.**
2. **Leash:** monsters return home if beyond legal territory; recommend no teleport and no implicit resurrection or auto-drop. **OPEN HP regenerate/reset rule.**
3. **Elite/Boss:** reuse authentic V2 authored skills/tier; introduce attack phases only when real skill/content exists. **OPEN boss-specific aggro/territory and spawn window.**
4. **Mob clustering:** allow real fights against multiple mobs; avoid unseen whole-map dogpile by bounded perception. **OPEN max engaged enemies or spawn density**, balance after playtests.
5. **Multi-team Boss:** share one HP and one Sect loot; **OPEN participation/loot/individual credit**. Do not invent last-hit ownership.
6. **Offline:** event-driven hibernation allowed only with same authoritative outcomes; **OPEN cap/processing backlog behavior**.
7. **Production sourcing:** do not move synthetic per-kill loot rates into game; catalog import revisions and exact source names must be approved before CODE.
8. **Gameplay progression:** hunt vs Base economy remains complementary; injury/return/recovery real, not fake instant HP refill on Boss reset.

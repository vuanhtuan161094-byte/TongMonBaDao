## Non-negotiable effect identity

- All V6.3 poison stacks: owner actor ID, skill generation, application event ID, attack snapshot policy, element shares, expires_at, tick schedule; 5 per owner per target, 10s baseline, Human/Immortal/Divine damage 6%/12%/20% of source ATK per second (candidate).
- Effective Boss stacks: 1–12 at 100%, 13–24 at 50%, 25+ at 25%; **30 actual become 19.5 effective**; allocate diminished effective damage fairly across owners, deterministic independent of application order. Do NOT truncate owned stack count or grant extra damage when source dies.
- Blood Demon: Immortal 75% original HP active ceiling, allied heal only eligible when below 40% original HP at 60% effectiveness; Divine 65% ceiling, allied heal below 35% at 50%; no ordinary self heal/lifesteal; Blood sacrifice pays current actual HP and earns bounded Blood Marks. Rare covenant 55% HP ceiling, no in-combat healing, shield effectiveness -40%, +20% direct fist damage. Blood Life branch exempt.
- Fatal-hit protection uses per-recipient family ICD 90s; no resurrection of Injury target; no perpetual invulnerability.
- A skill descendant's exclusive effect replaces predecessor, never stacks across Human/Immortal/Divine generations.
- A boss-specific phase modifier is triggered by **event**, not auto-activated by just being Divine.

### Boss stage / cast contract

By default thresholds P1 100–70, P2 70–35, P3 below35. Threshold crossed event fires **once per spawn_generation**, even if Boss heals. Summons are bounded max 4 alive/Boss; telegraph always has explicit geometry and >=1 viable safe/mitigation route. Conditional break pressure applies only to tagged interruptible casts; otherwise skill executes normal fallback direct/control. Boss encounter must not reuse noncontinuous 90s timeout for 150–240s challenge fights. Wilderness may use 55–70% of challenge HP when balanced; does not copy boss HP scaling from current party.

### Growth and numerical policy

CombatStatsV2.derive uses G=(1.65^(realm-1))*(1.03^(floor-1)) separately for cultivation/body; monster catalogue today uses a related G but pilot factors are not final bosses. Author zone realm/floor and monster curves explicitly. 40.16 billion HP Divine at 30-10 is a **provisional fixture**, NOT validated from real DPS; do not mark as locked endgame. Damage order unchanged from CombatStatsV2. All bonus % apply once per declared effect channel. Fight simulations need evidence and real map movement before numeric lock.

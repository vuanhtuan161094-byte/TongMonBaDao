- **Cultivation track:** Tu Vi has **30 realms×10 floors**; Luyện Thể separately **30×10**, not forced identical. Coordinates `r=1..30` and `f=1..10`; `global_floor=(r−1)×10+f`. ## 5. Cross-generation transition without destroying 2/3/4 sets
Example `R10 Nhân Trấn Yêu` full four items → `R11 Tiên Trấn Yêu`: collect/source NEW Tiên inputs and 4 correct lineage+slot templates; forge/loot target pieces into Sect Warehouse and verify wearer realm, skill and source. Preserve old worn Nhân four-piece and all investments while preparing. When legal same-generation Tiên group meets owner selected threshold (recommended 4 complete for a full4 swap), use ONE grouped assignment transaction or explicit checked sequence with no intermediate combat exploit. The generation transition has actual item cost; unlike `r=9→10`, it may need a new unique target instance and compatible inheritance credit. Donor consumption must wait until ready, never automatically duplicate or trash a high-value old item. If player requests incremental 2+2 while gearing, preserve only the actually legal remaining threshold and disclose loss of4-tier bonus, don't silently count mixed-generation four.
**An old high-grade Nhân piece cannot turn into Tiên simply because the wearer reaches Tu Vi11.** Same for Tiên20→Thần21. Existing body gate seals are **not** a Forge input by virtue of being registered. A 1000–3000 Sect can hold many old items as hand-me-downs instead of 1000 copies of a new Boss-only template.
